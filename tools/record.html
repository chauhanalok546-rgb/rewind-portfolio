Rewind: voice recorder

Record each line in your own voice. Quiet room, same microphone throughout, normal speaking distance (a hand's width from the mic). Speak warmly and confidently, like telling a friend. If you slip, just record the line again.

ARM-01 lines (amber tag) are the robot. Record them too in your own voice, short and flat; the site turns them into a synthetic radio voice. You can also skip them and keep the placeholder robot voice.
